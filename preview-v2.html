Understanding your export limit

Your maximum instantaneous net grid export power is 3.5 kW. This is a power limit, not a daily energy limit.

Exporting at 3.5 kW for 30 minutes produces 1.75 kWh. Maintaining that rate for four hours would produce 14 kWh, provided sufficient energy is available.

The planned battery export shown above is the total energy (kWh) across scheduled export periods. Solar generation and household demand must also be considered when calculating net power exported to the grid.
